Your safety comes first

Safety Centre

Whether you are meeting someone, exploring a public profile or discussing a service, protect your privacy and respect other people's boundaries. Velora's safety guidance does not replace emergency services or personal judgment.

In immediate danger?

Move to a safe public place if possible and contact local emergency services. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance. Do not confront a threatening person if doing so could put you at risk.

🔐

Protect your account

  • Use a unique, strong password.
  • Never share passwords or one-time verification codes.
  • Do not sign in through suspicious links.
  • Sign out on devices you do not control.
🪪

Watch for fake profiles

  • Be cautious if someone's details keep changing.
  • Do not assume a profile is verified unless the platform actually verifies it.
  • Never share private documents with strangers.
  • Be careful when someone quickly asks to move to another app.
🤝

Consent and boundaries

  • Every interaction must be voluntary.
  • Ask before sharing photos or personal information.
  • Anyone can change their mind or withdraw consent.
  • Never pressure, threaten or harass another person.
💳

Avoid payment scams

  • Do not send money because someone threatens or pressures you.
  • Confirm fees, cancellation terms and refund conditions in advance.
  • Never share UPI PINs, card PINs or banking OTPs.
  • Keep transaction records if you pay for a legitimate service.
📍

Meeting in person

  • Choose a public place for an initial meeting.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going.
  • Arrange your own transport when possible.
  • Leave if you feel unsafe or pressured.
📷

Photos and privacy

  • Only upload images you have permission to share.
  • Never distribute intimate images without consent.
  • Avoid posting your home address or daily routine.
  • Remember that screenshots and copies can be difficult to remove.

Report suspicious behaviour

If you encounter harassment, impersonation, threats, suspected exploitation or a scam, preserve relevant evidence when safe and report it to the platform operator. For serious threats or suspected crimes, contact the appropriate authorities.

The reporting feature and official support contact must be configured before this page can accept real reports.
