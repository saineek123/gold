Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains the intended handling of personal information on Velora. The actual data collected and processing performed depend on the features enabled on the live website. Review and update this policy to match the real implementation before launching the platform.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the platform may process:

  • Account details, such as name and email address.
  • Public profile information you choose to publish.
  • Booking requests and related service information, if that feature is enabled.
  • Messages you submit to support, if a support feature is provided.
  • Technical information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

Do not submit passwords, bank credentials, government identity numbers or other highly sensitive information in public profile fields.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Creating and maintaining your account.
  • Showing profile details that you intentionally publish publicly.
  • Operating requested features and responding to support requests.
  • Protecting users, investigating abuse and securing the platform.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

3. Firebase and Service Providers

Velora is being developed using Firebase services for account authentication and database functionality. Information may be processed by the relevant service providers to deliver those features, subject to their applicable terms and privacy documentation.

The platform operator should identify the services actually enabled, review their data-processing terms and provide any additional required disclosures before launch.

4. Public Profiles

Information in a public profile may be visible to other visitors when the profile is published. Do not publish your home address, private phone number, financial details or other information you do not want strangers to see.

A profile should only be publicly displayed through an explicitly configured public listing. Private account records should not be used as public profile data.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect personal information. These include appropriate database access rules, restricted administrative permissions and secure account authentication.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Security measures should be reviewed and tested as features are added.

6. Data Sharing

Information should only be shared when necessary to operate requested features, protect users, comply with legal obligations or otherwise as permitted by applicable law. The operator should document the actual recipients and circumstances of any data sharing.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information should be retained only for as long as needed for disclosed purposes, legitimate operational requirements and applicable legal obligations. A process for account deletion and handling related records should be implemented before launch.

Deleting an account may not automatically delete every related database record unless the deletion process is specifically implemented.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. The platform operator should provide a working contact method and respond to valid requests within applicable timelines.

9. Children's Privacy

Velora is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The platform should not knowingly allow minors to create accounts or publish profiles. An age checkbox alone is not proof of age; appropriate safeguards and reporting procedures are needed.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage or similar technologies if enabled by its implementation. This policy should be updated to identify the actual technologies used, their purposes and any available controls.

11. Policy Updates

This policy may change when the service, its features or legal requirements change. The latest version and revision date should be published here.

12. Contact and Privacy Requests

Add the platform operator's official name and working support email or contact form here before launch. Do not publish placeholder contact details as if they were real.
